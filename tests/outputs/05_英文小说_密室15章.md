# Aragog (Chamber of Secrets, Ch. 15) · Knowledge Cards (8)

### 1. The School in Crisis

**Core Knowledge:** With Dumbledore suspended by the governors and Hagrid taken to Azkaban as the suspected attacker, Hogwarts is gripped by fear.

**Concise Explanation:** Visitors to the hospital wing are barred ("there's every chance the attacker might come back to finish these people off"), students are shepherded from class to class in packs, and laughter in the corridors is quickly stifled. Harry keeps repeating Dumbledore's parting words — help will always be given to those who ask for it.

**Example or Self-Question:** Why were Harry and Ron unable to visit Hermione in the hospital wing?
Answer: Madam Pomfrey barred visitors, fearing the attacker might return to finish off the victims.

### 2. Following the Spiders

**Core Knowledge:** Acting on Hagrid's hint, Harry and Ron plan to track the spiders at night, following them into the Forbidden Forest using the Invisibility Cloak and taking Fang along.

**Concise Explanation:** The spiders move in an unnaturally straight line "as though taking the shortest route to a prearranged meeting", clearly heading for the Forbidden Forest. Harry argues the centaurs and unicorns make it less dangerous; Ron is terrified but agrees after glancing at Hermione's empty seat.

**Example or Self-Question:** Where were the spiders heading, and what did Harry intend to do about it?
Answer: They were heading for the Forbidden Forest; Harry planned to follow them at night under the Invisibility Cloak with Fang.

### 3. The Weasleys' Car Gone Wild

**Core Knowledge:** Deep in the forest, Harry and Ron find Mr. Weasley's car standing empty in a clearing — "the forest's turned it wild", and it now roams the forest on its own.

**Concise Explanation:** The car's sides are scratched and smeared with mud, its headlights blazing; it moves toward Ron "exactly like a large, turquoise dog greeting its owner". Fang is terrified of it, and the headlights scare away the spiders, temporarily losing the trail.

**Example or Self-Question:** What did Harry and Ron unexpectedly find in the forest clearing?
Answer: Mr. Weasley's car, which had gone wild and was roaming the forest by itself.

### 4. Aragog's True Identity

**Core Knowledge:** Aragog is not the monster of the Chamber of Secrets; he comes from a distant land and was given to Hagrid as an egg, secretly raised by young Hagrid in a cupboard in the castle.

**Concise Explanation:** "I was not born in the castle. I come from a distant land. A traveler gave me to Hagrid when I was an egg." Young Hagrid cared for him in a hidden cupboard, fed him scraps, later found him a wife (Mosag), and the spider family grew "all through Hagrid's goodness".

**Example or Self-Question:** Where did Aragog come from, and how did Hagrid raise him?
Answer: He came from a distant land, given to Hagrid as an egg and raised secretly in a castle cupboard.

### 5. Aragog Never Harmed Humans

**Core Knowledge:** Out of respect for Hagrid, Aragog has never attacked anyone; years ago he was falsely blamed for the death of a girl, which is why Hagrid was expelled and Aragog moved to the forest.

**Concise Explanation:** "It would have been my instinct, but out of respect for Hagrid, I never harmed a human." He only ever saw the cupboard he grew up in, never the rest of the castle. The murdered girl's body was discovered in a bathroom.

**Example or Self-Question:** Why was Aragog believed to be the monster of the Chamber years ago?
Answer: He was discovered and blamed for the death of a girl; Hagrid protected him and he has lived in the forest ever since.

### 6. The Unnamed Creature

**Core Knowledge:** The creature living in the castle is feared above all others even by spiders, and Aragog refuses to name it — he never even told Hagrid.

**Concise Explanation:** "We do not speak of it! We do not name it!" — the spiders clearly dread the thing that moves about the school. Harry senses it is "a sort of monster Voldemort", something even other monsters dare not name.

**Example or Self-Question:** Did Aragog tell Harry what creature lives in the castle?
Answer: No. He refused to speak of it or name it, and had never even told Hagrid.

### 7. Rescue by the Car

**Core Knowledge:** Just as the spiders close in to feed on Harry and Ron, Mr. Weasley's car comes thundering down the slope, scatters the spiders, and carries the boys out of the forest.

**Concise Explanation:** The car drives itself — doors fly open, the engine roars without anyone touching the accelerator, and it "winds its way cleverly through the widest gaps, following a path it obviously knew", smashing through the undergrowth until it stops at the forest's edge.

**Example or Self-Question:** What saved Harry and Ron when the spiders closed in?
Answer: Mr. Weasley's car, which drove itself down the slope, knocked the spiders aside, and carried them out of the forest.

### 8. The Key Clue — Moaning Myrtle

**Core Knowledge:** Harry reasons that the girl who died was found in a bathroom — "What if she never left the bathroom? What if she's still there?" — which points to Moaning Myrtle, the ghost haunting the girls' bathroom.

**Concise Explanation:** This idea strikes Harry as "their very last hope": if the victim's ghost never left the place where she died, the girl in the bathroom could still be there to question — a direct new lead after every other trail has hit a dead end.

**Example or Self-Question:** Who does Harry suspect the girl who died in the bathroom might be?
Answer: Moaning Myrtle, the ghost who haunts the girls' bathroom.
